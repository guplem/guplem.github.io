Sudoku Screenshot Coach

Drop in a screenshot with a sudoku somewhere in it. The tool finds the grid, reads the digits, and tells you the next move to make, and why that move is forced.

1. Load a puzzle

Drop a screenshot, paste it, or choose a file

The sudoku can sit anywhere in the picture, next to anything else. Press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) to paste one straight from the clipboard.

What the reader saw

2. Check the grid

Select a cell and type a digit to fix anything the reader got wrong. Press Backspace to empty a cell. Cells the reader was unsure about are marked.

The tool never reads the pencil marks in your picture. It works these notes out from the grid, and it applies every elimination it can prove, so its list is often shorter than the one your sudoku app shows.

3. Get the move

The techniques it knows

The coach always offers the easiest technique that works on your grid, the way a teacher would. These are the ones it can find and explain, from the simplest to the hardest.

The puzzle is read in your browser. Your picture never leaves your device and is never uploaded.
