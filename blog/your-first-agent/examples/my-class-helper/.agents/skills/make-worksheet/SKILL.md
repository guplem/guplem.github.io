# Make a worksheet

1. Ask me the group and the topic, if I have not said so.
2. Read data/groups.md to know the level of the group and what it finds hardest.
3. Write 8 exercises, from easiest to hardest. The first two come with a worked example.
4. Save the worksheet in exports/worksheet-GROUP-TOPIC.html, with no spaces or accents in the name (for example worksheet-4B-fractions.html), with large letters and space to answer, and the answers on a separate page.
5. Tell me how to print it: open it in the browser and use Print.
