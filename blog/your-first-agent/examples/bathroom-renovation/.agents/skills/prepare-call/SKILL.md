# Prepare a call

1. Ask me who I am going to talk to, if I have not said so.
2. Read data/steps.md, data/contacts.md and data/budget.csv.
3. Write a short list: what I must tell them, what I must ask them and what we must decide.
4. Save it in exports/call-PROFESSIONAL-DATE.md.
5. After the call, ask me what we discussed and update data/contacts.md and data/steps.md.
