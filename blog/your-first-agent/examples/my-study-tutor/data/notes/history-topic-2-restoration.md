# History, topic 2: The Restoration in Spain

- It starts in 1874 with King Alfonso XII. The Constitution of 1876 sets up the system.
- The political leader is Cánovas del Castillo.
- Two parties take turns in power: the Conservative (Cánovas) and the Liberal (Sagasta).
- The turns are guaranteed by caciquismo (local bosses) and by fraud in the elections.
- In 1898 Spain loses Cuba, Puerto Rico and the Philippines. This is "the Disaster".
- After the Disaster, intellectuals ask for change. This is regenerationism.
