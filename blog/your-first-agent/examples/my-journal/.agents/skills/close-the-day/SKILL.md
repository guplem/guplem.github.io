# Close the day

1. If I have not told you about my day, ask me: "How did today go?".
2. Listen. Ask me two questions at most to understand it better.
3. Save the entry in data/entries/DATE.md in the format that the AGENTS.md gives: the date, the mood and what I told you, in my own words.
4. If a person or a topic comes up that is already in data/themes.md, add one mention to it. If it is new and I have named it twice, add it.
5. Say goodbye with a short sentence.
