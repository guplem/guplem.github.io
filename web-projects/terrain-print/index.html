      Data

      –

      Terrain Print turns real elevation data into 3D-printable models. Choose an area of the Moon, Mars or Earth, then download one tile, pieces that fit your printer, or a jigsaw puzzle, with an optional frame. On Earth and Mars you can set the sea level.

      Heights: NASA LRO LOLA and JAXA SELENE Kaguya (Moon), NASA MGS MOLA and ESA Mars Express HRSC (Mars), both via USGS Astrogeology and Esri; AWS Terrain Tiles by Mapzen with SRTM, GEBCO and others (Earth). Maps: NASA Solar System Treks, OpenStreetMap contributors. Drawn with Leaflet and three.js.
