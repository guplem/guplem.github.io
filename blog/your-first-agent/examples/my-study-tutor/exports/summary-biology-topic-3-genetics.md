# One-page summary: Genetics (Biology, topic 3)

This is the topic you find hardest. I took it from your notes.

## The key words
- **Gene**: a piece of DNA that gives an instruction, like eye colour.
- **Alleles**: the two copies of a gene, one from each parent.
- **Dominant (A)**: always shows. **Recessive (a)**: only shows if there are two (aa).
- **Genotype**: the alleles you have (Aa). **Phenotype**: what you can see.

## Mendel's laws
1. **First law**: AA x aa gives children that are all Aa. All of them look like the dominant one.
2. **Second law**: Aa x Aa gives one aa child in every four. The recessive trait comes back.

## The Punnett square
It is a table with all the possible children. For Aa x Aa:

| | A | a |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | AA | Aa |
| **a** | Aa | aa |

Result: 25% AA, 50% Aa, 25% aa.

## What is hard for you: two genes
In AaBb x AaBb, the phenotype comes out in the ratio **9:3:3:1**.
Tip: first make one square for gene A and one for gene B. Then put the results together.
